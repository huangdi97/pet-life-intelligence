## Product

- Name: PLI — Pet Life Intelligence. Chinese owner product: 宠物生命智能.
- One sentence: PLI is the life interface of **one specific pet** — 豆豆 (demo Corgi),
  and the pet is the identity, not a data subject inside a pet-management app.
- Audience scene: a pet owner at home, phone in hand, opening the app to "see 豆豆
  right now" — check current state, see what changed, see evidence, take the next step.
  Warm, calm, personal time; one hand; glances and 2-second interactions.

## Unique mechanism

Pet → Now → Change → Attention → Action → Memory. The 3D pet is the composition
center; state anchors, changes, evidence, and memory are arranged **in spatial
relation to the pet**, never as a dashboard grid below a hero card.

## First surface to prove (P0)

Today (Spatial Living Canvas), Pet (Pet World), Life View (Full 3D Living Stage).
They must make the user feel: "This is 豆豆, existing in a space, right now."

## Mode

Operate / Experience hybrid: the visitor is completing the task "see my pet now",
but presence and warmth carry the experience. Expression must never obscure the
state or the task.
